I observed that human perception, human reasoning, human planning, human execution, human navigation, human action, human talking, human writing share some similarities to transformer.
1. Perception starts with a goal. in perception, human eyes will saccade. The sccades is a process that goes through a sequence of successive saccade. Latter step is not planned beforehand, and it depends on previous steps. The sequence stops when perception concludes or the process in interrupted.
2. Reason starts with a goal. in human reasoning, human mind will run a flow of thoughts. The reasoning is a process that goes through a sequence of thoughts with or without backtrack. Latter thought is not planned beforehand, and it depends on previous thoughts. The sequence stops when a conclustion is made or the process is interrupted.
3. Human planning starts with a goal. in human planning, human will run a flow of planning steps. It is a process that goes throught a sequence of steps with or without backtracks. Latter step is not planned beforehand, and it depends on previous steps. The process stops when a conclusion is made or the process is interrupted.
4. Human execution or navigation start with a goal. in human execution or human navigation, they may or may not have a plan or map, but they also run a flow of steps. It is a process that goes through a sequence of small decision and actions with or without backtracks. Latter step is guided by original plan/map but still refers to previous steps. The process stops when a conclusion is made or the process in interrupted.
5. Human action start with a goal. in human action, human might a rough idea beforehand but they don't know the exact sequencee of moves beforehand. It is a process that goes through a sequencee of subactions with or without backtracks. Latter subaction depends on previous subactions. The process stops when a conclustion is made or the process is interrupted. 
6. human talking or writing start with a goal. In talking or writing,  human doesn't know the latter words they will speak or write in the beginning. It is a proces that goes through a sequence of words with or without backtracks. Latter word is not planned beforehand but generated in the fly based on previous words. The process stops when a conclusion is made or the process in interrupted.
7. transform is next token prediction machanism. It has been applied to texts, speech, audio. video, actions. The mechanism is to generate a sequence of tokens, and latter tokens depend on previously generated tokens. The process stops when a conclustion is made, and can be interrupted by injecting new information to its context window.
All these processes all have goals, run throught a sequential process with or without backtracks, and will be concluded or intertupted. And all the processes can handle interruptions, accidents, and off-track events while attending to relevant information and ignoring unrelevant informations.
